Tensions observed synthesized

T-contrast

The brand blue fails AA as text

#438ece is 3.5:1 on white, used for every link and news headline, and as the fill under 13px white nav labels. AA needs 4.5:1. Direct must decide which captured colour carries text.

Source: _brand-extraction.json § palette[cnv-blue].contrastOnWhite

T-no-headings

No semantic headings

0 h1–h6 on the homepage; titles are styled divs and links. Screen-reader users get no outline. The target needs one h1 and an h2 per module.
