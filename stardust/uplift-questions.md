# "What if…" candidates — https://www.cnv.org/

Cinematic register for C (Phase 3a): **arrival**. PRODUCT.md Brand Commitments
read civic + place-led (municipal service portal; community pride in named
places: the Shipyards, Lonsdale, the waterfront) → `civic-formal` +
`place-led` → `arrival` per motion-registers.md § Selection heuristic. No
signage/monogram signature, no live data, not editorial — no tie to break.

## Picked

### Variant C · Audience-routing reframe (civic arrival)
Source: catalog (#7)
What if: "What if the homepage arrived like the city does — the aerial view
settling into place, then the six things residents come to do rising to meet
you?"
Cinematic register: arrival
Evidence: pages/index.json § ctas — above the fold the page carries 3 utility
CTAs + 6 nav sections + a 4-slide carousel (> 4 intents, none of them a task);
online services appear only as the "ONLINE SERVICES" utility link; captured
aerial photography (_brand-extraction.json § photography[0]) is the arrival
image.
Motion bet: a slow arrival sequence (hero photo scale-settle, headline and
service search rise in, task tiles stagger up) then calm scroll reveals; all
of it removed under prefers-reduced-motion.

### Variant B · Photography re-foregrounding
Source: catalog (#2)
What if: "What if North Vancouver's own photography carried the page —
the places residents know, at editorial scale?"
Captured trait amplified: the City's own place photography
Evidence: _brand-extraction.json § photography — 1800×460 aerial and
Shipyards originals rendered as a 368px black-stage strip; 344×198 feature
photos at 276px wide; all own photography, none stock.
Composition bet: a split hero (large photograph + navy text panel), a
photo-led "Around the City" mosaic replacing the four equal cards, and news
told with a lead story image — the IA order shifts so place and community lead
while services stay one click away in the hero panel.

## Disqualified

- **Display-typography amplification** — disqualified: the only family is
  Open Sans (a generic workhorse, no display family, no signature axis).
- **Live-data promotion** — disqualified: the captured homepage exposes no
  live operational data (no counters, wait times, status boards); faking
  liveness would be invention.
- **Signature-gesture extension** — disqualified: no captured signature
  gesture beyond the logo's small blue swash; no monogram/illustration motif
  appears on the page.
- **Motif vocabulary swap** — disqualified: the page has one primitive per
  module (cards, lists) and no under-used alternate primitive to promote.

## Considered but not picked

- **Color-ladder re-weighting** (navy `#00467f` used only as the footer fill,
  T-color-imbalance) — strong trigger, but every variant already moves text
  and the nav bar to navy for contrast (improvement 2), so it is applied as a
  fix in A rather than spent as B's bet. B uses navy panels as part of its
  photo-led composition.
- **Voice-register pivot** (civic-formal vs community-warm: "Get Ready for
  Winter at The Shipyards" vs "Official results announced…") — real contrast,
  but weaker evidence (4 headlines) than photography; B lets warmer section
  titles ride along with the photography.
